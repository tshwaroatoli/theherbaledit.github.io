About Us

The Herbal Edit is a small, student-run business that creates natural, affordable herbal drinks, wellness hampers and tasty bakes and snacks. We believe in the power of nature to support your health, boost your energy and help you feel your best, inside and out.

Our Mission

To provide natural, high-quality herbal drinks and wellness products that are affordable, accessible and beneficial for our customers’ health and well-being.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and recognised herbal wellness brand that inspires healthier lifestyles, supports sustainable practices and makes natural care part of everyday life.
